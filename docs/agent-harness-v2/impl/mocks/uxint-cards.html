#Problem
1The question asked first renders under the later approvals (fixed slots in chat-response.tsx:710-775).
2Cards sit under the timeline, not at the step that asked; when the run ends they jump into the collapsed section.
3A batch of N identical calls gives N cards and N clicks; nothing groups them.
4Deciding the 2nd card of a batch first does nothing visible: the run waits on the 1st (driver.py:2963-2972).
5Decided cards stay full size until the run ends; after a run, a decided card disappears from view.

7No count of what waits, no focus move, no announcement; buttons are 36px on phones.
8The hold expires after 120 s with no sign on the card; the run is suspended while the UI still shows "running".
